Join the BMT Group

Thank you for your interest in the BMT Group! We are always looking for highly motivated individuals to joint our group or collaborate with us. Please read the following sections before contacting us.

Ph.D. and M.Phil. Students

You are encouraged to apply to the MPhil-PhD in Mechanical and Automation Engineering program at CUHK. In your application, please make sure to mention Prof. Samuel Au. If you would like to email us during the application process, please use the subject Prospective Ph.D./M.Phil. Student and include your CV, transcript, a brief statement of your research interests, why you are interested in our group, and any other relevant materials you want to share in the email.

M.Sc. Students

If you are a current M.Sc. student in the Department of Mechanical and Automation Engineering at CUHK (M.Sc. in Mechanical and Automation Engineering or M.Sc. in Robotics) and are interested in participating in our research, please enroll course M.Sc. Project (MAEG/ROSE 5190) and select a research project posted by our group.

Undergraduate Students

If you are a current final-year undergraduate student at CUHK majoring in Mechanical and Automation Engineering, you are welcome to join our group for your Final Year Project (MAEG 4998, 4999). For undergraduate students in other years, please send us an email with the subject Prospective Undergraduate Student. Please include your CV, transcript and other materials you want to share in the email. In general, we only consider those who can spend a summer in the lab on research.

Postdocs and Visiting Students

Please send us an email with the subject Prospective Postdoc/Visiting Student. Please include your CV, transcript, research experience, why you are interested in our group, and other materials you want to share in the email.

Collaborators

Thank you for considering collaborating with us! Please send us an email with your background, which direction/project/paper you would like to collaborate on, and other materials you want to share in the email.

Contact

Please send your email to Prof. Samuel Au (samuelau@cuhk.edu.hk). For prompt response, you may also send a copy to Prof. Xiangyu Chu (xiangyuchu@cuhk.edu.hk).
