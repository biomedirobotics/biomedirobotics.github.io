Pillar I

Biorobotics

Novel mechanisms and control methods that exploit the natural dynamics of the robot.

1Bioinspired Agile Locomotion

Borrowing animals' tricks for leaping, falling and landing.

Key directions

  • Aerial reorientation learned from kangaroo rats
  • Morphable tails for self-righting and safe landing
  • Hopping robots: OMEGA, SIGMA and tailed bipeds

Selected work

  • Aerial reorientation in kangaroo ratsICB 2024
  • 3-DoF morphable inertial tailRA-L 2024
  • OMEGA: open-source hopping platformRA-L 2025

2Control of Underactuated Systems

Robust, real-time control that keeps robots stable in the wild.

Key directions

  • Null-space avoidance for underactuated robots
  • QP control for reorientation and contact forces
  • Nonholonomic vehicles and rigid-body attitude

Selected work

  • Null-space avoidance frameworkTCST 2022
  • Tail and reaction wheel reorientationRA-L 2023
  • Safe landing of falling quadrupedsICRA 2023
Pillar II

Medical Technology

Built with surgeons and industry, from the bench toward products.

3Dexterous Surgical Instruments

Bendable instruments for confined surgical spaces.

Key directions

  • Inherently safe organ retraction via beam buckling
  • Steerable drills for spine and bone surgery
  • Continuum endoscopes with shared control

Selected work

  • Handheld steerable surgical drillTBME 2022
  • Robotic endoscope for sinus surgeryT-MECH 2022
  • Organ retraction and space openingUS Patent 2025

4Surgical Autonomy and Robot Learning

Perception and decisions that let robots do surgical tasks.

Key directions

  • Visuomotor policies and world models for grasping
  • Robotic ultrasound: needle detection and alignment
  • Deformable and dexterous object manipulation

Selected work

  • Surgical embodied intelligenceSci. Robot. 2025
  • World models for surgical graspingRSS 2024
  • VibNet: needle detection in ultrasoundTMI 2025
