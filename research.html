The Bioinspired Robotics and Medical Technology (BMT) group develops innovative robotic systems for medical applications and agile locomotion. One research pillar is to develop robots with greater agility, efficiency, and robustness comparable to their biological counterparts: we develop new methods of design, analysis, and optimization of novel robots and mechanisms, and design algorithms that exploit mechanical properties to achieve exceptional physical performance. The other pillar is to improve safety, accessibility to confined spaces, and efficacy for robotic-assisted surgery, working with industrial partners, technical experts, and physicians to translate these technologies into products that meet real-world medical needs.
